The Core Asset: Contact Graph

The prototype's moat isn't the UI — it's a unified graph of who VCET knows, how warm, and what they need or offer. This is what the Clean House Agent is really building.

Entities & edges

Person — identity (email-first), org, roles, VCET tags (Investor · Mentor · Partner · Founder · Job Seeker · Member · Alumni) + affinity tags (Software · Vermont · UVM · Middlebury · Boston · Serial Entrepreneur …).

Interaction — every email, meeting, note, and intro as a timestamped edge with source reference.

Needs / Offers ledger — what each contact seeks or can give, across the six match verticals; captured at meeting download.

Warmth, made honest

Warmth(person, owner) = recency × frequency × channel mix × reciprocity — computed per owner, so Dave-warmth and Nicole-warmth are distinct, with overlap surfaced.

Offline reality check: warmth is adjustable. One tap to boost or demote ("we're closer than the data shows") — the human correction trains the graph. We show the score as a signal, never as truth.

"I'm closer with people on LinkedIn than HubSpot — more personal connection vs. just logging emails."— Nicole, discovery
07

Trust Architecture — Dave-Proofing

Dave's ranking says matching first, but his quotes say trust decides adoption. These are commitments, not features:

Grounded in truth

Every claim carries a citation that click-throughs to the HubSpot record, email, or note it came from. No source → the Brain says "I don't know." Accuracy eval set built from 25 gold questions Dave & Nicole write themselves — we publish the score to them.

Human in the loop

Nothing auto-sends. Matches are suggestions with a validate step before any intro. The system makes them faster, not lazier — their judgment is the product's core, ours amplifies it.

Data stays theirs

Deployed into VCET's own Google Cloud project. LLM calls run zero-retention; no training on their data. Attachments and pitch decks are excluded from the index by default — opt-in only, per Dave's exact concern.

No lock-in

Full graph export (JSON/CSV) at any time; model-agnostic LLM adapter; connectors read from their systems of record rather than replacing them. The requirement from the Evolution frame, honored in the design.

"Don't want to give Claude someone else's deck — I don't know where it goes." · "Don't want it to make me lazy — gotta be putting in the brainpower."— Dave, discovery

Top Risks & Mitigations

R1  LinkedIn depth

Their warmest data lives where we can't API. Mitigate: CSV snapshots + our own interaction-derived warmth; be transparent; revisit browser-assist in v2 only if the gap actually hurts match quality.

R2  HubSpot data quality

Garbage in, distrust out — and Dave is accuracy-obsessed. Mitigate: Clean House completes before graph build; Nicole QA session gates the demo; eval score shown, not hidden.

R3  Trust in suggestions

Discovery flagged it: "Will they trust the bot? Will they validate?" Mitigate: citations-or-silence, validate-before-intro flow, warmth override — the system defers to them visibly.

R4  Scope creep

Eighteen ranked needs, five weeks. Mitigate: v0 line frozen today; everything new routes to v1.1/roadmap; Lisa holds the line as delivery DRI.
